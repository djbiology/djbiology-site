Alkaline protease

The Specialist for Stubborn Protein Stains

Recommended dosage0.2 – 1.5 % w/w

Target stains

Blood · dairy residues · egg stains · meat gravies & broth · fermented soybean sauces · perspiration proteins & keratin dead-skin deposits

Applications

Concentrated liquid detergents, detergent powders, laundry pods, color-safe bleaches, pre-wash spotters

Washing data

On standard protein-soiled cloth JB02 at 30 °C, the detergency index improves 18% versus a commercially available protease — 6.5% at 15 °C and 9.8% at 20 °C. Granules stored 14 days at 37 °C / 75% RH retain about 87% of initial enzyme activity, against about 69% for the commercial reference.

Key benefits

  • Superior detergency performance. Selectively catalyzes the hydrolysis of proteins into water-soluble peptides and amino acids, effectively removing stubborn proteinaceous soils such as blood, milk, sweat, and egg residues.
  • Action on aged & heat-set soils. Exhibits powerful micro-penetration and soil-lifting capabilities against aged, sun-exposed, oxidized, and heat-set protein soils that have deeply bonded with fabric fibers.
  • Outstanding formulation stability. Highly tolerant to surfactant and chelating agent systems, maintaining robust, long-lasting biocatalytic activity across a broad temperature range and within complex detergent matrices.
  • Gentle fiber care. Protects the natural structure of cellulosic fibers like cotton and linen without harsh chemical residues, preserving fabric softness wash after wash.
  • Multi-enzyme synergistic system. Forms a robust catalytic synergy with amylases and lipases, effortlessly breaking down complex composite everyday soils.

Target stains

Applications

Key benefits

  • Deep grease & lipid removal. Targeted biocatalytic breakdown of stubborn oily soils — including culinary oils, animal fats, sebum, and lipstick — delivering deep-cleaning performance at the fiber level.
  • Cold-water performance. Retains high catalytic activity in low-temperature wash environments, significantly enhancing cold-water degreasing efficiency while minimizing washing energy consumption.
  • Fabric protection & anti-yellowing. Mild, highly selective catalysis leaves no harsh chemical residues, effectively preventing fabric yellowing and fiber embrittlement caused by residual lipid oxidation.
  • Multi-enzyme synergy. Exhibits outstanding compatibility and synergistic effects with proteases and amylases, delivering comprehensive, all-round fabric care and cleaning solutions.

Amylase

The Starch Stain Specialist

Recommended dosage0.05 – 0.4 % w/w

Target stains

Rice & pasta residues · porridge & congee · mashed potatoes · thickened sauces & gravies · infant starch cereals

Applications

Liquid detergents, detergent powders, laundry pods, pre-wash spot removers

Washing data

On Chinese National Standard starch-soiled cloth JB05, our amylase reaches a detergency ratio of 1.65 — about 1.54× the detergency of an enzyme-free laundry powder (0.65).

Key benefits

  • Targeted starch soil removal. Efficiently hydrolyzes stubborn starchy residues — rice grains, noodles, mashed potatoes, porridge, and baby food — rapidly dismantling sticky starch matrices to achieve deep cleaning at the fiber level.
  • Breakdown of aged & gelatinized matrices. Delivers outstanding penetration and soil-lifting capabilities against heat-gelatinized, dried-on, and aged starch crusts, while degrading macromolecular starches into soluble fragments to prevent soil redeposition.
  • Low dosage & high formulation compatibility. Exceptional specific catalytic activity boosts stain removal at ultra-low inclusion levels, with robust tolerance across concentrated liquids, powders, and complex detergent systems.
  • Cold-water energy-saving catalysis. Superior catalytic activity under low-temperature wash conditions, aligning with modern cold-water wash cycles to reduce overall washing energy consumption.
  • Multi-enzyme synergistic system. Excellent compatibility and synergy with proteases, lipases, and cellulases, comprehensively dismantling complex everyday composite soils.

Cellulase

The Specialist in Fabric Renewal & Biopolishing

Recommended dosage0.15 – 1.0 % w/w

Fabric care targets

Surface pilling & fuzz · fabric fading & greying · fiber stiffness & rigidity · trapped micro-particulate soils

Applications

Cotton liquid detergents, color-care laundry pods, fabric conditioners, anti-pilling pre-wash treatments

Key benefits

  • Mild biopolishing & color care. Gently catalyzes the selective cleavage of damaged microfibrils, delivering exceptional color retention, anti-pilling performance, and a smooth fabric surface.
  • Deep soil release & particulate removal. Completely dismantles the fuzzy microfibril network, effectively releasing and lifting deep-seated particulate soils trapped within fiber micropores.
  • Enhanced hand-feel & natural softness. Significantly improves fabric hand by reversing fiber stiffness and hardening caused by repeated washing, keeping garments naturally soft and skin-friendly.
  • Tailored for cellulosic fabrics. Engineered specifically for cotton and cellulosic-blend laundry detergents and premium fabric conditioners, providing comprehensive fabric rejuvenation and extended garment lifespan.
